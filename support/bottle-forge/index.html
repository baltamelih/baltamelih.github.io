Frequently Asked Questions

My progress is not appearing on another device

Bottle Forge uses an anonymous account system. Progress may not automatically transfer between devices unless both installations are associated with the same Firebase user session.

Advertisements are not loading

Advertisement availability depends on your region, internet connection, consent settings and advertiser demand. The game remains playable when an ad is unavailable.

Rewarded advertising did not grant a reward

Ensure the advertisement completed fully. If the issue continues, restart the application and contact support with your device model and friend code.

My leaderboard score is not updating

Scores are first saved locally and then synchronized with Firebase. Check your internet connection and reopen the leaderboard after a short period.

How can I delete my data?

Send an email with the subject “Bottle Forge Data Deletion” and include your friend code or player identifier.
